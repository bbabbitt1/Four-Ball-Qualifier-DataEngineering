Each stat is converted to a z-score across that championship's alternates and flipped so higher is better, then combined using your weights (normalized, so they don't have to add up to 100). A missing value counts as average. Filtering to 1st or 2nd alternates ranks that group on its own. Alt: the site's 1st or 2nd alternate; "?" marks the few BlueGolf sites that list two alternates tied with no order. Δ is the move versus the default weights. Field z: gross vs that site's field (negative = better). WAGR SoF: ranked-player strength of the site's field vs the season (positive = stronger). Field to-par: the site's mean to-par vs the season (negative = tougher).
